    what's in the mock

    1. One pager. The usual week is page zero; › pages into this week, next week and on. The label is always two lines, so nothing jumps. Tap it for a week picker: a small calendar whose rows are the weeks, with orange dots on away days. A dated page turns the label and the sticky header's rule orange, nothing filled.
    2. The deal. On a lens change the columns re-deal left to right, and the date numbers drop into the header. Off under reduced motion.
    3. Ink follows the lens. free in the usual week, away in a dated week, solid orange by default (the hatch toggle above is there to compare; stripes are the one non-color cue for red-green colorblind readers). Drag a rectangle; it becomes one away entry, Mon to Wed between 9 and 5 style.
    4. Tap a day header in a dated week for all-day away. An all-day column is locked: any tap clears the whole day. A note lives inside its own orange zone, down a single day or across a span, clipped to the zone; headers stay number and weekday. Zones under three hours carry no label. Anything before 7am or after 11pm has no cell, which is the one thing the form could say and the grid can't; I'd accept that.
    5. Notes on the grid. Marking away drops a chip under the cells: the range and a note field. Enter or "add" saves; Escape or the next mark dismisses. The away list under the grid is the record: click an entry to jump to its week, edit its note, remove. The date form is gone.
    6. Autosave. Two seconds after you stop, the status line at the bottom posts. No post button.
    7. Friend view tab: the same orange away, an > edit button on your own page, copy link right-aligned.
    8. Proposed change surfaced by the mock: the usual week runs Monday to Sunday like the friend view, so the columns keep their identity across lenses.
